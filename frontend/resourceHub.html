Welcome to the Novice Teacher Resource Hub

A digital resource hub for novice and student teachers working with young English language learners.

Browse Resources Open AI Assistant

How This Hub Helps Teachers

Literacy Resources

Teachers can browse approved literacy resources, instructional strategies, lessons, and scholarly information.

Grade Level Curriculum

Curriculum resources can be organized by grade level so teachers can quickly find information that applies to the students they are teaching.

AI Literacy Assistant

Teachers can ask literacy related questions and receive suggestions based on approved curriculum and project resources. The final instructional decision still belongs to the teacher.

Supporting Teacher Decision Making

The AI assistant is meant to make lesson planning easier by helping teachers find useful information faster while still staying within the approved literacy curriculum.
